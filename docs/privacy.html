1. Scope, Purpose & Who We Are

Welcome to Crickdin (also known as crickedIn, package com.crickdin), the premier Cricket Operating System ("Cricket OS"). This Privacy Policy sets forth our clear, transparent standards regarding how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal information when you access our Android mobile application, web applications, and associated services.

By installing, signing into, or using Crickdin, you consent to the practices described in this document. If you do not agree with any provision, you may adjust your in-app privacy toggles or discontinue using the platform at any time.

Core Philosophy Crickdin operates on the principle of progressive consent and minimal necessary access. We collect only what is essential to provide live ball-by-ball scoring, player hiring, venue booking, and community highlights.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

Crickdin requests Android runtime permissions only when an action directly requires it. You can revoke any permission at any time through your device settings:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used in our Video Studio to record match action, bowling actions, boundary highlights, and to snap profile photos. Camera is never accessed in the background.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly during video recording to capture live match atmosphere and audio commentary.
  • Media & Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Allows you to pick match photos and cricket reels from your device gallery to publish to your profile or feed.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used exclusively while searching for nearby turfs, nets, and active matches in your city.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Delivers live score updates, team match requests, tournament schedule shifts, and booking confirmations.

4. Payments & Financial Security

When booking a cricket turf, registering for a paid tournament, or using Split-Pay, payments are routed through PhonePe Payment Gateway, a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment aggregator:

  • No Storage of Sensitive Card/PIN Data: Crickdin does NOT store, log, or transmit your credit card numbers, CVV, debit card PIN, or UPI MPIN on our servers.
  • Encrypted Tokens: Transaction authentication occurs entirely inside PhonePe's encrypted sandbox or your native UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm).
  • Transaction Records: We retain only standard non-sensitive reconciliation details: Order ID, Payment Gateway Transaction Reference, Amount Paid, Timestamp, and Slot Reservation Status.
PCI-DSS Compliant Infrastructure All payment transactions meet PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance requirements managed by certified payment aggregators.

6. How We Use & Process Your Data

We process your data strictly under valid legal bases (contract performance, legitimate interests, and explicit consent) to:

  1. Power Ball-by-Ball Live Scoring: Synchronize match events offline-first, updating Net Run Rates (NRR), tournament knockout trees, and Orange/Purple cap leaderboards.
  2. Facilitate Talent Matching: Match captains in need of 11th-hour replacement players with verified local cricketers based on skill attributes.
  3. Enable Turf Reservations: Confirm turf slots with ground owners and verify attendance check-ins.
  4. Deliver Social Community Features: Distribute cricket reels, match photos, and tournament awards on the community feed.
  5. Maintain Fair Play & Security: Prevent multi-team tournament impersonation, cheating, and fraudulent payments.

7. Third-Party Partners & Cloud Infrastructure

We rely on battle-tested, enterprise-grade cloud partners who are bound by confidentiality and strict data processing agreements:

  • Supabase Inc. (Database & Authentication): Cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict tenant isolation.
  • PhonePe (Payment Gateway): RBI-licensed payment infrastructure for handling slot bookings and tournament entry fees.
  • Google Play Services / Firebase: Crashlytics for real-time crash diagnosis and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications.
  • Cloudflare / Global CDN: Secure, cached delivery of video highlights and match images.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile and media for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership of your data and can execute your right to be forgotten:

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. Inside the Crickdin App: Navigate to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account. Confirm with your registered credentials.
  2. Via Web / Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to privacy@crickdin.com with the subject line "Delete My Crickdin Account".
Impact of Account Deletion Account deletion permanently wipes your profile photos, video clips, private messages, and contact details within 30 days. Historical match records (e.g. "Player scored 45 in Match #102") remain anonymized to preserve match scorecard integrity for both teams.

9. Children & Minor Safety

Crickdin is designed for sports participants aged 13 and above (or 16 and above where required by regional laws). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental or academy guardian consent. If we learn a minor has registered without guardian consent, we promptly remove the record.

10. Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy multi-layered safeguards to keep your sports records resilient against breaches:

  • In-Transit Encryption: All client-to-server traffic is enforced with modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with HSTS.
  • At-Rest Encryption: All database stores and media volumes utilize AES-256 storage encryption.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Granular SQL access policies ensure users can only modify their own profile data, posts, and authorized scoring matches.
  • Continuous Vulnerability Auditing: Regular automated security scanning of backend endpoints and mobile application builds.
