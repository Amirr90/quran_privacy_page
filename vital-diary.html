Al-Quran

Read the Quran, track progress, and stay connected with family.

About the app

Al-Quran is a free app designed to bring the Holy Quran closer to you every day. Whether you want to read at your own pace, listen to beautiful recitations, or keep track of your progress, the app is built to support your journey.

You can read the full Quran text with translations in multiple languages, bookmark verses, follow reading plans, and share with family through circles—all in one place.

Made with care for the Muslim community.

Our mission

We aim to make the Quran accessible, easy to read, and easy to live with. The app is built to work offline where possible, so you can read and listen even without an internet connection. We respect your privacy and do not use your reading data for advertising.

Features

  • Full Quran text – Complete Arabic text with Uthmani script and multiple translations
  • Audio recitations – Listen to renowned reciters; stream or download for offline use
  • Bookmarks and progress – Save verses and track your reading across devices when signed in
  • Prayer times and Qibla – Accurate prayer times and a Qibla compass
  • Duas and Dhikr – Collections of supplications and remembrances
  • Family circles – Stay connected with family and share progress or verses
  • Reading plans and Juz – Structure your reading by Juz or follow a plan
  • Search – Find verses and topics quickly

Translations and recitations

Quran text and translations are sourced from trusted, well-known editions. Audio recitations are provided by respected reciters and platforms. We are grateful to the scholars, translators, and reciters who make this content available.

If you have feedback about a translation or recitation, please get in touch through the app or the contact details below.

How to get the most from the app

  • Set your preferred translation and font size in Settings.
  • Enable prayer times and allow location (optional) for accurate Adhan times.
  • Use bookmarks to revisit verses and sync them by signing in.
  • Join or create a family circle to share progress with loved ones.

Contact and feedback

We welcome your feedback, suggestions, and reports of any issues. For general support, privacy questions, or to report a problem, please use the contact option in the app or reach out via the store listing.

Credits and acknowledgments

We thank the providers of Quran text, translations, and recitations. This app would not be possible without their work and the broader open and licensed resources that serve the Ummah.
