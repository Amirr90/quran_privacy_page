Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026 · App: VitalDiary (com.vitalDiary)

VitalDiary (“we”, “our”, or “us”) helps you record health vitals, medicines, and family profiles. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the VitalDiary Android application and related services linked from the app (including this page). It does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not control.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use VitalDiary, we may process:

  • Account information — phone number (OTP sign-in), Google account details used for sign-in, and basic profile fields such as name, date of birth, gender, height, weight, blood group, and profile photo.
  • Health and medical information — vital readings you enter (for example blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, temperature, SpO₂, weight), notes, timestamps, allergies/conditions you add, and medicine details (name, dose, schedule, reminder settings, and dose taken/missed logs).
  • Family member profiles — names, relationships, and related profile/health data you choose to store for family members.
  • Emergency contact information — phone numbers and related contact details you provide.
  • App preferences — theme, language, notification and reminder settings, units, and onboarding choices stored on your device.
  • Device and advertising data — advertising identifiers and limited device/app signals used by Google AdMob and consent tooling where permitted by law and your choices.

We do not ask for payment card details in the free ad-supported experience. If paid features are offered later, payment processors will handle payment data under their own policies.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and secure your account and sync your data across devices when signed in
  • Show your vitals history, charts, medicine schedules, and reminders
  • Support family profiles and emergency contact features you enable
  • Remember your app settings and improve reliability and usability
  • Show ads (and, where allowed, personalized ads) to keep the free app available
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations

VitalDiary is a personal health diary tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your device — local storage (including Room database and preferences) so core features can work offline.
  • In the cloud — when you are signed in, account and synced data may be stored with our backend provider, Supabase (authentication and database services).

Access to cloud data is restricted by authentication and access rules designed so that only your account can access your records.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

VitalDiary uses Google AdMob to display ads (for example banner ads) so we can offer the app for free. AdMob and Google may collect or use identifiers such as your advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data to serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads.

In regions that require it (for example the EEA/UK), we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) so you can make privacy choices before personalized ads are shown. You can also reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Google Ads settings / advertising ID controls on Android).

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

6. Third-party services

We use trusted providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud database
  • Google Sign-In — optional account authentication
  • Google AdMob / UMP — advertising and consent messaging

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services to us.

7. How we share information

We do not sell your health records. We share information only when:

  • Needed to operate the app through the providers listed above
  • You ask us to (for example support requests you send by email)
  • Required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • Needed to protect users, the public, or VitalDiary against harm or abuse

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Local data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

You can delete your account from the app profile settings. You may also email support@vitaldiary.app from the address or phone number linked to your account. We will process verified requests within a reasonable time, subject to legal retention requirements.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (including ads consent)
  • Export a copy of your data where technically feasible

To exercise these rights, contact support@vitaldiary.app. You can also manage notification permissions and advertising preferences in your device settings.

10. Children’s privacy

VitalDiary is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including authenticated access to cloud data and secure transport. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your device and account credentials.

12. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of VitalDiary after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes may also be highlighted in the app or on our website.

14. Contact us

Questions about privacy or this policy:
Email: support@vitaldiary.app
Website: https://vitaldiary.app
